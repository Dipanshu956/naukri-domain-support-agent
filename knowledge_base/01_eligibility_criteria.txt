Job applicants must have at least a bachelor's degree or an equivalent qualification relevant to the advertised role, unless the job description specifies a different requirement. Most professional roles require at least one year of relevant experience, while entry-level roles may accept fresh graduates. Current employees may apply for open positions if they meet the stated qualifications and are not under an active disciplinary process. Applicants must be legally eligible to work in the location specified for the role and must be able to meet the role's stated joining requirements.
